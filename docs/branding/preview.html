Three logo concepts for the app, each as an app icon (16 / 32 / 128 px, the 16 and 32 also pixel-doubled) and a wordmark on dark and on light, for both candidate names. Open README.md for the rationale and the shortlist.

Verse

Three centred lyric lines; the gold one is the line being sung now (the Live line). Product-literal: what the congregation sees. Sources: docs/branding/verse/icon.svg, wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-light.svg.

Responserecommended

A double quotation mark: the sung words. The first stroke is white (the call), the second gold (the response). Name-literal for Antiphon, in the spirit of Canticle. Sources: docs/branding/response/icon.svg, wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-light.svg.

Window

A church arch in white with a gold light inside: the nave, the sanctuary door, light in the room. Place-literal. Sources: docs/branding/window/icon.svg, wordmark-dark.svg, wordmark-light.svg.
