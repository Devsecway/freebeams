FFmpeg in FreeBeams
===================

This copy of FreeBeams includes the programs ffmpeg and ffprobe from the
FFmpeg project (https://ffmpeg.org/), with the FFmpeg libraries they share
(avcodec, avformat, avfilter, avutil, swscale, swresample: the avcodec-*.dll
... files on Windows, libavcodec.so.* ... on Linux). FreeBeams runs the
programs as separate processes to read the metadata of videos and audio
files, to make poster frames, and to decode videos and audio for playback.

You may replace ffmpeg / ffprobe and these libraries with any other build.
